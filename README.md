# DreamPath

An AI platform enabling college students to maximize their post-grad potential by optimizing the college experience.

Outfitted with an agentic system that generates dynamic recommendations for students, DreamPath guides students with 3 core pillars of college: 

1. **Courses** — the courses you take, within and outside of your major(s) and minor(s)
2. **Clubs** — your on-campus extracurricular activities
3. **Career** – your personal alumni network
